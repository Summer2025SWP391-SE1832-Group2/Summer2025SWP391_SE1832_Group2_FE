ADN Testing Service Management System

✨ Project Overview

This software system supports a medical facility in managing its DNA paternity testing services. It enables streamlined service booking, sample collection, test tracking, and result delivery for civil and administrative DNA testing.

👨‍🧱 User Roles

Guest: Browse public content and learn about services.

Customer: Register, book services, track tests, and view results.

Staff: Collect samples, update test progress, and manage requests.

Manager: Monitor staff, feedback, and reports.

Admin: Full control over system settings, services, and users.

🏥 Core Features

1. Homepage & Informational Content

Facility and service introduction

ADN testing guidance

Educational blog articles

2. Service Booking & Sample Collection Options

Self-sampling at home

Staff-collected sampling:

At the customer's home

At the medical facility

Service options depend on the type of ADN test

3. Civil ADN Testing Workflow (Self-Sampling)

Register Service Appointment
→ Receive Sampling Kit
→ Collect Sample
→ Send Sample to Medical Facility
→ Perform Testing
→ Record and Deliver Results

4. In-Facility ADN Testing Workflow

Register Service Appointment
→ Staff Collects Sample & Updates Request
→ Perform Testing
→ Record and Deliver Results

5. Result Management

View results securely via user dashboard

Exportable test reports (PDF or web view)

6. Service & Pricing Management

Manage services and categories

Update pricing and descriptions

7. Feedback & Rating

Customer feedback and service ratings

Ratings visible to management for quality control

8. User Profile & Booking History

Manage personal data

View past tests and results

9. Dashboard & Reports

Analytics for test volume, feedback, revenue

Role-based access to reporting tools

📌 Non-functional Requirements

Secure handling of personal and health data

Responsive, mobile-friendly UI

Scalable and maintainable architecture

Role-based permissions and logging
